For company administrators

Set up the company workspace

Complete the company settings before sending a real project to a client. These settings control pricing, access, and accounting handoffs throughout the app.

  1. 1

    Confirm the company

    Open Company from the main navigation and select the company workspace.

  2. 2

    Set the tax rate

    Open Tax settings and enter the default rate used when new estimates and invoices are created.

  3. 3

    Create cost codes

    Open Cost codes and add the product, material, labor, subcontractor, and other codes used for project pricing.

  4. 4

    Invite the team

    Use Invite team member to assign each person an internal role. Project access is assigned separately after a project is created.

  5. 5

    Connect QuickBooks when ready

    A company administrator can connect the correct QuickBooks company and complete customer and item mappings before invoice synchronization.

Recommended order

Create the first project

A small amount of setup gives the project team and client a clear workspace from the beginning.

  1. 1

    Create the project

    From Projects, select New project. Add the project name, internal code, status, description, start date, and target completion date.

  2. 2

    Assign team access

    Open the project and select People and access. Choose each internal user and decide whether they can manage workflows, invite clients, and receive project email notifications.

  3. 3

    Invite the client

    Send the invitation to the exact email address the client will use. Invitations expire after seven days and can be resent from the same screen.

  4. 4

    Add the starting information

    Upload current plans and documents, create the estimate, add open selections, and start any project conversations the client should see.

  5. 5

    Check the portal

    Review client visibility before sending approvals. Draft estimates, draft change orders, internal documents, cost details, and the schedule stay out of the client portal.

Project work

Use the dashboard and project modules

The Projects dashboard shows the jobs you can access. The Action center brings pending decisions, unfinished drafts, delays, and open conversations into one list.

Messages

Keep project questions and replies in searchable conversation threads. Close a thread when the question is resolved.

Documents

Upload plans, contracts, photos, and other files. Choose client visibility and whether a decision is required. New versions preserve the history.

Estimates

Create the estimate, add cost-coded line items, and submit it to the client. The first approved estimate sets the fixed project contract amount.

Selections

Add allowances and finish options, then publish the selection. The app flags overages and credits after the client chooses.

Change orders

Document scope, pricing, cost, and schedule changes. Add line items, submit the draft, and keep the authenticated client decision in the project history.

Invoices

Create a draft manually or from approved project work, issue it to the client, download the PDF, and track the balance and payment status.

Schedule

Internal project users can track milestones and delays. Clients do not have access to the project schedule.

Financials

Review contract, change orders, selections, invoices, payments, and balances. Internal budget and margin details remain restricted by role.

For homeowners and clients

Use the client portal

The portal contains only the projects and information assigned to the signed-in client.

Join the project

  1. Open the invitation email and follow the secure link.
  2. Use the invited email address and create a password if the account is new.
  3. Open the project from the Projects dashboard.
  4. Start with Action center to see decisions that need attention.

What a client can do

  • Read and reply to project messages.
  • View shared files and upload documents or photos.
  • Approve or decline documents, estimates, and change orders.
  • Choose published finish selections.
  • View issued invoices, PDFs, balances, payments, and the client-safe financial summary.

After a decision: Treat an approval or selection as final. Contact the project team through Messages if something needs to be revised or reopened.

Company administrators

Connect and synchronize QuickBooks

QuickBooks remains the accounting source of truth. Current synchronization actions are deliberate and administrator controlled.

  1. 1

    Connect the company

    Open QuickBooks, sign in to Intuit, and select the correct company.

  2. 2

    Map the project customer

    Match or create the QuickBooks customer that represents the construction project. The app does not depend on QuickBooks Jobs.

  3. 3

    Map cost code items

    Match each cost code used on invoice lines to a QuickBooks Product or Service item before sending the invoice.

  4. 4

    Send the invoice

    Issue the invoice in Construction Manager, then use the invoice detail page to synchronize it to QuickBooks.

  5. 5

    Bring payments back

    Use Sync payments from QuickBooks on the invoice to update local payment records and balance status.

  6. 6

    Resolve exceptions

    Review failed or blocked synchronization attempts on the QuickBooks screen. Confirm mappings and accounting data before retrying.

Current payment workflow: Clients can view invoices and payment status, but they cannot pay online through this app.

Access control

Understand roles and project permissions

A company role identifies the user's general responsibility. Project assignment controls which jobs that person can open, and project permissions control what management actions appear.

RoleTypical access
AdminAll company projects, team administration, company settings, QuickBooks, and subscription controls.
Manager and Project managerAssigned projects and project workflows. These roles can create projects; project-level permissions still determine management and client-invitation access.
Staff, Office manager, and Real estate agentAssigned projects. Available actions depend on the permissions selected under People and access.
AccountantAssigned financial records and accounting work without client messages, documents, the action center, or the schedule.
ClientOnly assigned client projects and client-visible information. Internal costs, margins, drafts, and the schedule remain hidden.

Before sending work

Check these common first-use details

  • Confirm the client-visible setting before uploading a file.
  • Submit or publish drafts when they are ready; clients cannot see unfinished drafts.
  • Use the exact invitation email the client or team member will use to sign in.
  • Map every invoice cost code to a QuickBooks item before synchronization.
  • Keep files at or below 25 MB and use a supported document or image format.
  • Review the Action center before each client update so pending decisions and drafts are not missed.
