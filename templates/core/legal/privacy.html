Privacy Policy

Effective date: {{ legal_effective_date }}

This Privacy Policy explains how {{ legal_business_name }} (“Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, retains, and protects information when you use Construction Manager (the “Service”). It applies to contractors, employees, project personnel, clients, and other authorized users. It should be read with our Terms of Use and End-User License Agreement.

1. Information we collect

Account and organization information

We collect names, email addresses, authentication records, organization and role information, project assignments, invitation status, account settings, and communications with support. Passwords are stored only in one-way hashed form; we cannot retrieve your original password.

Construction and project information

Authorized users may submit project names and addresses, schedules, milestones, messages, documents, photographs, plans, selections, allowances, pricing, estimated costs, change orders, approvals, comments, client decisions, and related metadata. The Service records activity and timestamps to provide an audit history and protect project integrity.

QuickBooks Online information

If an authorized organization connects QuickBooks Online, we may receive the QuickBooks company identifier, OAuth authorization tokens, customer records, invoices, credit memos, payments, transaction identifiers, sync status, and related accounting fields selected for integration. We do not request or store your Intuit password. The exact information available depends on the permissions approved during Intuit’s authorization flow and the features your organization uses.

Technical and security information

We may collect IP address, browser and device information, session and CSRF security cookies, request timestamps, login successes and failures, application errors, and diagnostic logs. We currently use essential cookies for authentication, security, and application operation and do not use third-party advertising cookies.

2. Sources of information

We receive information directly from you, from your employer or project organization, from other authorized project participants, automatically through use of the Service, and from services you authorize us to connect, including Intuit QuickBooks Online.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • create accounts, authenticate users, and enforce organization and project permissions;
  • provide project communication, document, selection, change-order, scheduling, notification, and audit features;
  • exchange authorized accounting information with QuickBooks Online and identify or resolve synchronization errors;
  • deliver emails and other service communications requested by users or administrators;
  • secure, troubleshoot, maintain, and improve the Service;
  • respond to support, privacy, and legal requests; and
  • comply with law, enforce agreements, and protect users, Company, and others.

We do not use QuickBooks data or project content for third-party advertising, sell it to data brokers, or use it for a purpose materially different from providing and securing the Service without additional notice and any consent required by law.

4. How information is disclosed

We may disclose information as follows:

  • Within your organization and projects. Information is available to users according to roles, explicit project assignments, client visibility settings, and workflow permissions.
  • At your direction. We transmit information when an authorized user publishes, approves, uploads, exports, notifies, or synchronizes it.
  • Service providers. Hosting, database, storage, cache, email, security, monitoring, and support providers may process information for us under contractual confidentiality and data-protection obligations.
  • QuickBooks Online. When authorized, we exchange selected information with Intuit to provide integration features. Intuit handles information under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Legal and safety reasons. We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law or valid process, enforce agreements, investigate fraud or security events, or protect rights and safety.
  • Business transactions. Information may transfer as part of a merger, financing, reorganization, or sale, subject to appropriate confidentiality and notice obligations.

We do not sell personal information for money or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

5. QuickBooks authorization and control

A QuickBooks administrator chooses whether to connect a company and sees the permissions requested through Intuit’s OAuth consent flow. We access QuickBooks information only within that authorization and only for enabled features. Authorized administrators may revoke access through the Service, through their Intuit account, or by contacting us.

Disconnecting QuickBooks stops new API collection and synchronization after the revocation is processed. We disable and securely delete active OAuth tokens within 30 days of a confirmed disconnect. Data already synchronized into project or accounting records is not silently removed because doing so could damage required business records; an authorized organization administrator may request its export or deletion as described below.

6. Retention and deletion

Our retention practices are:

  • We retain account and project information while the applicable customer relationship or project workspace remains active and as directed by the organization controlling that workspace.
  • After a verified deletion request or termination of the applicable service agreement, we delete or de-identify covered personal information and QuickBooks-derived data from active systems within 90 days, unless an authorized customer asks us to retain it or law requires retention.
  • Residual copies may remain in protected backups for up to an additional 90 days and are not restored except for disaster recovery, security, or legal purposes.
  • Financial, contractual, payment, audit, fraud-prevention, and dispute records may be retained longer when reasonably necessary, including for applicable tax, accounting, construction, insurance, limitation-period, or other legal requirements. Access remains restricted during that period.
  • Security logs are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to detect, investigate, and document security events and enforce the Service.

To request deletion, an authorized administrator should email {{ legal_contact_email }}. We will verify identity and authority, identify any records that must be retained, and confirm completion or explain an applicable exception.

7. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed for the nature of the information, including access controls, project-level authorization, password hashing, transport encryption, private document storage, login-abuse controls, audit records, and restricted operational access. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Please promptly report suspected unauthorized access to {{ legal_contact_email }}.

8. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on your location and relationship with the organization using the Service, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or withdrawal of consent. Many project details can be corrected through an authorized organization administrator. You may also contact us using the information below. We may verify your identity, authority, and jurisdiction before acting. You may appeal a denial by replying to our decision. You will not be discriminated against for exercising applicable privacy rights.

If your account is managed by an employer, contractor, or other customer, that organization may control the relevant project records. We may direct your request to that organization or assist it in responding.

9. International processing

The Service and its providers may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate. Where required, we use contractual and other safeguards intended to protect information transferred across borders.

10. Children

The Service is intended for business and project use by adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has submitted information so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

11. External services and links

Third-party services, including Intuit, operate under their own privacy policies. This policy does not govern a third party’s independent handling of information after you choose to interact with that service.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in law, technology, providers, or the Service. We will post the revised effective date and provide additional notice of material changes when required. We will request new consent before materially expanding use of information when applicable law requires it.
