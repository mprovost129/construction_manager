  1. Confirm the computer and administrator. Work on the Windows computer that runs QuickBooks and can open the company file. Arrange for the QuickBooks administrator to approve the application; enter passwords privately on that computer.
  2. Check storage and prepare a backup. Have IT verify the active company file’s location. If its path contains OneDrive, confirm whether that folder is being synchronized before proceeding. Do not move an open company file. Make a current backup and prepare a separate test company.
  3. Review existing connections. Check existing integrated applications with the accountant so a second connection is not created accidentally. Leave other working applications in place.
  4. Prepare Intuit Web Connector. Use Intuit’s official instructions and a version compatible with your installed QuickBooks release. The connection computer must be available when synchronization runs. Web Connector contacts Heritage 360 over an outgoing secure connection; do not expose the company file to the internet.
  5. Agree on the accounting details. Record how customers/jobs, items, accounts, taxes, invoice numbers, and credits will be matched. Use descriptions here, without passwords, license numbers, recovery codes, or copies of the company file.
  6. Save and open connection setup. An authorized installer can prepare the Web Connector file and verify the intended company without creating financial transactions. Invoice and credit transfers require further development, separate test-company validation, and accountant review before live use.
