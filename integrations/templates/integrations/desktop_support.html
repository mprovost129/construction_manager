Heritage 360 QuickBooks connection help

This service verifies a QuickBooks Desktop company file. It does not transfer invoices, credits or payments.

  1. Sign in to Heritage 360 and open QuickBooks → Connect QuickBooks Desktop.
  2. An administrator must grant Manage QuickBooks connection to the person preparing setup.
  3. Prepare the connection, download its .qwc file, and add it to Intuit Web Connector on the QuickBooks computer.
  4. Use the dedicated Web Connector password chosen during setup. Open the intended company, select the Heritage 360 row and click Update Selected.
  5. Refresh the connection page to read the result. If it fails, use the displayed instructions. Share only the error code with support, never passwords or unredacted QuickBooks logs.

QuickBooks stores app registration information when the administrator approves it. No financial records are created by this verification.
